    About

    About this prototype

    Real ships report their position by radio (AIS). This page shows those reports live from aisstream.io, relayed by the I'M HERE server. Pick a ship from the list to go aboard: stand on its deck, take the captain's view from the bridge, or follow it with the chase cam.

    Old Hickory Lake & small boats

    Most small pleasure boats – bass boats, pontoons, jet skis – do not carry AIS, so they never show up. On the Cumberland River / Old Hickory you will mostly see commercial barge tows and workboats, and sometimes nothing for a while. Busy ports (Los Angeles, New York, Houston, Gibraltar) show far more traffic.

    Simulated sample

    Menu → Simulated sample plays three made-up tows/tugs moving along an approximate Cumberland River path near Old Hickory Dam. They are not real vessels or real positions: they never show "live", and the I'M HERE label's (i) details say SIMULATED SAMPLE.

    What is real and what is estimated

    Tap (i) on the I'M HERE label (top left) for what is live, estimated or simulated right now, where it comes from and how old each vessel's last report is. Ships are drawn as a typical vessel of their reported type (not the real ship's look), sized and turned from what each ship reports; the sea, waves, wakes and rocking are simulated; movement between reports is estimated.
