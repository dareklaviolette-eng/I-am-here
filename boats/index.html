About this prototype

Real ships report their position by radio (AIS). This page shows those reports live from aisstream.io, on a map or in a 3D "be there" view where you can ride along on any vessel.

Old Hickory Lake & small boats

Most small pleasure boats – bass boats, pontoons, jet skis – do not carry AIS, so they never show up. On the Cumberland River / Old Hickory you will mostly see commercial barge tows and workboats, and sometimes nothing for a while. Busy ports (New York, Houston, Gibraltar) show far more traffic.

Simulated sample

Without a key, the page plays a short simulated sample: three made-up tows/tugs moving along an approximate Cumberland River path near Old Hickory Dam. They are not real vessels or real positions: they never show LIVE, and the I'M HERE card's (i) panel says SIMULATED SAMPLE.

What is real and what is estimated

Tap Sources on the I'M HERE card (top left) for what is live, estimated or simulated right now, where it comes from and how old each vessel's last report is.
