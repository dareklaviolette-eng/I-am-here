    About this prototype

    Real ships report their position by radio (AIS). This page shows those reports live from aisstream.io, relayed by the I'M HERE server. Pick a ship from the list to go aboard. You start at the helm: the bridge windows ahead, and two screens on the console in front of you, a chart and the instruments (heading, course, speed, rate of turn, status, draft, destination and ETA, position). You can also stand at the bow or follow the ship with the chase cam. Everything works by pointing and selecting (a tap or click, a controller trigger, or a pinch). Top left: ☰ opens the sidebar (Ships nearby, Search, Departments, Settings); the "i'm here" tag opens the I'M HERE card under it; › slides the helm data out, ‹ folds it back and then hides everything but one faint dot (tap the dot to bring it back). Top right: POV (Helm, Bow, Aft, Port, Starboard, Chase, God's eye, Recenter, eye height) and Layers. Tap or point at any other ship for its mini card, then "Be there" to hop aboard. In VR these controls are mounted high on the ship, along the top of the bridge windows, and do not follow your head. Quest extras: A = next view, right thumbstick click = next ship; B or Y (or the card's Exit VR row) leaves VR.

    Depth, wind and tide

    AIS does not report water depth, so the screens say "charted depth coming soon" instead of a number. Charted depth and wind / tide will come from NOAA in a later version. The bridge screens are not for navigation.

    Old Hickory Lake & small boats

    Most small pleasure boats – bass boats, pontoons, jet skis – do not carry AIS, so they never show up. On the Cumberland River / Old Hickory you will mostly see commercial barge tows and workboats, and sometimes nothing for a while. Busy ports (Los Angeles, New York, Houston, Gibraltar) show far more traffic.

    Simulated sample

    Menu → Simulated sample plays three made-up tows/tugs moving along an approximate Cumberland River path near Old Hickory Dam. They are not real vessels or real positions: they never show LIVE: the I'M HERE card's signal reads SIM (amber) and its ⓘ Details say SIMULATED SAMPLE.

    What is real and what is estimated

    The I'M HERE card (under the tag at the top left) starts small; tap it to expand or minimize it. Its signal row says LIVE (green) only while fresh AIS reports arrive, otherwise HIST and the age of the last report; ⓘ Details has the full IMO and MMSI, sources and credits. The card's picture is an icon of the ship type, not a photo of the ship. Ships are drawn as a typical vessel of their reported type (not the real ship's look), sized and turned from what each ship reports; the sea, waves, wakes and rocking are simulated; movement between reports is estimated.
