About this model

This is an illustrative model, not a scan of any patient. What is simulated or simplified, and where each part comes from: tap the I'M HERE label in the top-left corner, then (i).

The I'M HERE vision: build this same ride from a patient's own imaging (CT, MRI or echo), so their doctor can take them through their heart - to show a leaky valve, a narrowed coronary artery or a planned repair, together.

What you are seeing

  • Blood path: venae cavae → right atrium → tricuspid valve → right ventricle → pulmonary valve → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary veins → left atrium → mitral valve → left ventricle → aortic valve → aorta.
  • Each beat: the atria squeeze first, then the ventricles. Mitral and tricuspid valves close as the ventricles start squeezing ("lub"); aortic and pulmonary valves close as they relax ("dub"). Left-side valves close a moment before right-side ones, as in a real heart.
  • Cell colour shows oxygen: dark red (oxygen-poor) turns bright red in the lungs. Blood is never blue.
  • Coronary arteries (on the outside view) feed the heart muscle; they start just above the aortic valve.

How to use

  • Ride: drag to look around; play/pause, previous/next stop; change the heart rate or use slow motion.
  • Narrator and captions: Mute silences the voice (captions keep going); CC turns the captions on or off. Where you are, in plain words, is on the I'M HERE label: tap it to show it again.
  • Outside: drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom. X-ray shows the chambers (blue = oxygen-poor, red = oxygen-rich).
  • VR: open in a headset browser and press Enter VR. Mute and CC are on the console below you.

For education only - not for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
